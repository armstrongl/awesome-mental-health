## Tools

- [Calm](https://www.calm.com/) - App for meditation, sleep stories, breathing exercises, and relaxation techniques.
- [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) - Guided meditation and mindfulness app with courses for stress, sleep, and focus.
- [Woebot](https://woebothealth.com/) - AI-powered mental health chatbot grounded in cognitive behavioral therapy techniques.
- [Daylio](https://daylio.net/) - Mood and activity tracker that helps identify patterns in emotional wellbeing.
- [Moodfit](https://www.getmoodfit.com/) - Mental health toolkit with mood tracking, CBT exercises, breathing, and gratitude journaling.
- [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/) - Free meditation app with a large library of guided meditations and community features.
- [Sanvello](https://www.sanvello.com/) - App offering CBT-based tools, mood tracking, guided journeys, and peer support.
- [Bearable](https://bearable.app/) - Health and mood tracker that helps correlate symptoms, medications, and lifestyle factors.
- [Finch](https://finchcare.com/) - Self-care pet app that encourages daily wellness routines through goal setting and check-ins.
- [7 Cups](https://www.7cups.com/) - Platform offering free emotional support through trained volunteer listeners and online therapy.

## Blogs

- [NAMI Blog](https://www.nami.org/Blogs) - National Alliance on Mental Illness blog covering personal stories, advocacy, and mental health education.
- [Zen Habits](https://zenhabits.net/) - Blog by Leo Babauta on mindfulness, simplicity, and building positive habits.
- [The Mighty](https://themighty.com/) - Community-driven platform sharing real stories about mental health, disability, and chronic illness.
- [Tiny Buddha](https://tinybuddha.com/) - Blog offering wisdom and practical advice on mindfulness, happiness, and personal growth.
- [Mark Manson](https://markmanson.net/archive) - Articles on emotional intelligence, resilience, and the psychology of living well.
- [Psych Central](https://psychcentral.com/) - Evidence-based mental health resource with articles, quizzes, and news on psychological wellbeing.
- [Greater Good Magazine](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/) - UC Berkeley publication covering the science of happiness, compassion, and meaningful life.
- [Mind (UK) Blog](https://www.mind.org.uk/information-support/) - Mental health charity providing information and support for a range of mental health conditions.
- [The Blurt Foundation](https://www.blurtitout.org/blog/) - Blog focused on depression awareness with practical self-care tips and personal stories.
- [Verywell Mind](https://www.verywellmind.com/) - Expert-reviewed articles on mental health conditions, therapy, self-improvement, and wellness.

## Books

- [Feeling Good: The New Mood Therapy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/46674.Feeling_Good) - Classic CBT book by David Burns for understanding and overcoming depression and anxiety.
- [The Body Keeps the Score](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/18693771-the-body-keeps-the-score) - Bessel van der Kolk's groundbreaking work on how trauma reshapes the body and mind.
- [Lost Connections](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34921573-lost-connections) - Johann Hari investigates the real causes of depression and unexpected solutions beyond medication.
- [Reasons to Stay Alive](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25733573-reasons-to-stay-alive) - Matt Haig's memoir about his experience with depression and finding reasons to keep going.
- [The Anxiety and Phobia Workbook](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/175041.The_Anxiety_and_Phobia_Workbook) - Comprehensive self-help workbook by Edmund Bourne with exercises and techniques for managing anxiety.
- [Atomic Habits](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/40121378-atomic-habits) - James Clear's guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones through small behavioral changes.
- [Man's Search for Meaning](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/4069.Man_s_Search_for_Meaning) - Viktor Frankl's profound account of finding purpose and meaning through suffering.
- [Maybe You Should Talk to Someone](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/37570546-maybe-you-should-talk-to-someone) - Lori Gottlieb's memoir exploring therapy from both the therapist's and patient's perspective.
- [The Happiness Trap](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3250347-the-happiness-trap) - Russ Harris introduces acceptance and commitment therapy as a path to a rich and meaningful life.
- [It's OK That You're Not OK](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34286108-it-s-ok-that-you-re-not-ok) - Megan Devine's compassionate guide to navigating grief that challenges the idea of moving on.

## Courses

- [The Science of Well-Being (Yale)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/the-science-of-well-being) - Free Coursera course by Prof. Laurie Santos on the psychology of happiness and positive behavior change.
- [De-Mystifying Mindfulness (Leiden University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/mindfulness) - Course exploring the science, history, and practice of mindfulness meditation.
- [Mental Health First Aid](https://www.mentalhealthfirstaid.org/) - Training program teaching skills to identify, understand, and respond to mental health challenges.
- [Managing Emotions in Times of Uncertainty and Stress (Yale)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/managing-emotions-uncertainty-stress) - Course on emotional intelligence and stress management strategies.
- [Positive Psychology (UPenn)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/positivepsychology) - Martin Seligman's specialization on the science of resilience, grit, and flourishing.
- [Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (Palouse)](https://palousemindfulness.com/) - Free online adaptation of the classic eight-week MBSR program developed by Jon Kabat-Zinn.
- [Buddhism and Modern Psychology (Princeton)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/science-of-meditation) - Course examining Buddhist concepts through the lens of modern psychology and neuroscience.

## Miscellaneous

- [Crisis Text Line](https://www.crisistextline.org/) - Free 24/7 text-based mental health support by texting HOME to 741741.
- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) - Free 24/7 support for people in distress via phone call, text, or chat by dialing 988.
- [SAMHSA National Helpline](https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline) - Free referral service for mental health and substance abuse treatment available 24/7.
- [Open Sourcing Mental Illness](https://osmihelp.org/) - Non-profit dedicated to raising awareness and changing the culture around mental health in tech.
- [Mental Health Hackers](https://www.mentalhealthhackers.org/) - Community at the intersection of information security and mental health advocacy.
- [if-me.org](https://www.if-me.org/) - Open-source mental health communication app for sharing experiences with trusted people.
- [TED Talks: Mental Health](https://www.ted.com/topics/mental+health) - Curated collection of TED talks on mental health, resilience, and emotional wellbeing.
- [MindShift CBT](https://www.anxietycanada.com/resources/mindshift-cbt/) - Free anxiety relief app by Anxiety Canada based on cognitive behavioral therapy strategies.
- [r/mentalhealth](https://www.reddit.com/r/mentalhealth/) - Reddit community for mental health discussion, peer support, and resource sharing.
- [Burnout Index](https://burnoutindex.yerbo.co/) - Free self-assessment tool to measure risk of work-related burnout.
